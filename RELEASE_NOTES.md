# words 1.8.0

This is a debug-signed Android test build.

## Highlights
- Refreshes the 2025 curriculum wordbook using structured source headwords and ECDICT data.
- Adds independent CET-4, CET-6, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, postgraduate, SAT, GMAT and NGSL/NAWL reference wordbooks.
- Preserves review progress and favorites for matching entries; obsolete entries are deactivated rather than deleted.
- Improves Quick Browse drag-following, directional card transitions and interrupted-gesture settling.
- Improves quiz feedback, structured explanations, POS-aware Chinese meaning formatting and in-place dictionary enrichment.
- Includes attribution and license notices for bundled open wordbook data.
