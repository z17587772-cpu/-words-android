Implemented in source (based on current main and v1.8.0 release notes):
- Quick Browse supports left/right horizontal card swipes with coordinated directional animation.
- Browse settings support none / hide English / hide Chinese masking.
- Daily Quiz retains the source question after choosing; correct and incorrect selections receive separate visual feedback.
- Tapping an option after answering opens the detail explanation; answers are persisted once.
- Explanation includes headword, core meaning, examples, optional translations, online definitions/forms/examples/synonyms/antonyms and source attribution.
- Local example translations are preferred; MyMemory machine-translation fallback is labelled and cached in SQLite.
- Sound and vibration feedback can be configured independently.
- Wordbooks include the 2025 curriculum pack, high-school extension, CET-4/6, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, postgraduate, SAT, GMAT and NGSL/NAWL resources.
- Wordbook updates preserve matching entries' review progress and favorites; obsolete entries are deactivated rather than physically deleted.
- POS and meaning formatting improvements are included in v1.8.0 release notes.
