Implemented in feature-branch source (not all runtime-verified):
- Automatic LibriVox metadata discovery and Internet Archive discovery.
- Internet Archive automatic offline download is limited to item-level CC0 1.0, CC BY 4.0, and CC BY-SA 4.0 metadata accepted by policy.
- LibriVox items remain rights-quarantined until item/territory permission is established.
- Daily WorkManager discovery, resumable backfill, stable provider-ID deduplication, and incremental discovery.
- Serial unmetered-network downloads, exact-size validation, SHA-256 verification, duration probing, and immutable local publication.
- Local discovered-resource library with search, downloaded/favorites filters, attribution/license links, offline playback, and playback resume.
- 512 MiB storage quota with protection for pinned and recently played resources.
- Reconciliation for missing/corrupt files, stale partial downloads, and orphan files.
- License-revocation protection and shared-file hash deduplication.
- Database schema v13.
- Formal lesson content packages, validation/import, Ed25519 signed-catalog verification, DownloadManager/WorkManager sync path, and strict new-lesson vs review separation exist in source.
- Daily new-lesson assignment uses stable episode identity and excludes previously assigned episodes; review is a separate 1/3/7/14-day queue.
