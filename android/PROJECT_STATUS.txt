Latest listening changes in feature branch:
- Quiz screen hides bottom navigation and ignores system Back until the assessment is completed.
- Previous-stage playback and TTS are paused before quiz display; no audio controls are offered in the quiz.
- Quiz prompts/options and explanation text are larger; evidence sentence includes its Chinese translation in both immediate feedback and final review.
- Daily course card typography and padding increased.
- Daily archive includes today's assignment; new daily/review starts clear stale archive assignment context.
- Main scroll surface is reused on refresh to reduce one-frame white flashes and preserve scroll continuity.
- Resource library now surfaces only resources with explicitly cleared offline-download rights; pending-review candidates remain in the database but are not presented as playable downloads.
- Failed downloads can be explicitly requeued; retry UI now supports both failed and expired resources.
- Download worker has longer network timeouts, identity transfer encoding, bounded transient retries and a progress heartbeat.
- Abandoned downloads are recovered after 45 minutes and returned to the queue; emulator regression coverage added.
- Terminal download failures no longer cancel the remaining serial download queue.
